This space is for all of us — seekers, skeptics, seasoned practitioners, and the simply curious. In a world that often feels

fragmented and transactional, our community offers a place to reconnect: with stillness, with tradition, and with each other.

Rooted in Tibetan Buddhist values of compassion, awareness, and interdependence, this is not a marketplace, but a meeting

ground. Here, we gather not to consume, but to share — experiences, questions, silence, and insight.

Whether you're looking to meditate with others, join thoughtful conversation, or simply feel less alone on your path, you are

welcome here. No background, belief, or level of experience required — only an open heart and a willingness to show up with

sincerity.

This community is built on respect, not hierarchy; on presence, not perfection.

Let’s walk this path together.

🧡 Community Team & Founding Members
This space was created through the collective intention of a few practitioners who believed that meditation and

Buddhist practice should be free, accessible, and rooted in spiritual integrity — not consumerism.

Here are some of the people who helped shape this community:

Tenzin Marlowe

Spiritual Mentor & Founding Member A lifelong student of Tibetan Buddhism and former monastic in the Nyingma tradition,

Tenzin brings a grounded, compassionate presence to everything he touches. He helps ensure that the community stays aligned

with the heart of the teachings, offering occasional talks and guiding silent retreats.

Amara Fields

Community Facilitator & Listening Circle Host

Amara is a trauma-informed yoga and meditation teacher who grew up in

Queens. She believes deeply in creating welcoming spaces for BIPOC and LGBTQ+ folks who’ve felt alienated by wellness

spaces. She leads monthly community sits and co-hosts the reflection circles.

Liam Chen

Events & Accessibility Coordinator

With a background in social work and a passion for urban dharma, Liam helps organize in-

person gatherings and works to make events accessible for all — including ASL interpreters, sliding scale donations (when

needed), and inclusive materials. He is currently training in socially engaged Buddhism.

Rina Das

Digital Steward & Forum Moderator

Rina is a software developer and lifelong seeker who designed the first version of the site.

She maintains our digital community spaces with kindness and care, ensuring respectful dialogue and a safe environment for

all members.

Michael “Miko” Alvarez

Practice Host & Peer Support Organizer Miko came to meditation through grief and community organizing. He now facilitates

peer-led meditations and support circles for people navigating stress, burnout, or loss. His warmth and humor make everyone

feel welcome — no robes or credentials needed.
