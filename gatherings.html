Our gatherings are simple, warm-hearted occasions for community — no agenda, no transaction, just the chance to sit together, eat together, and reconnect with what matters. Rooted in Tibetan Buddhist values of compassion and non-attachment, these events are open to all, whether you’re deeply practiced or just curious.

Come as you are.
